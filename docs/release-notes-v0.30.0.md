**Warrior rage now matches Forever's normalization — v0.30.0 is live**

**Warriors**
• Auto-attack rage no longer depends on damage dealt. Each landed swing gives base weapon speed × 3.46 rage for one-handers, or × 4.5 for two-handers. The off hand earns half that rate.
• Critical and glancing blows give the same rage as a normal hit. Misses, dodges and parries give none.
• Better weapon damage and more crit no longer mean more rage, so Warrior DPS drops noticeably on the default setups: Arms −17%, Fury −18%, Protection −7% threat.
• Warrior "Assumed Forever BiS" sets are rebuilt for the new rage model.

**Not changed yet:** rage from damage taken (tanks) and Bear rage still use the Classic formulas until there's better Forever data.

**Also:** the Zul'Gurub Edge of Madness charms (Gri'lek's, Hazza'rah's, Renataki's, Wushoolay's) are no longer picked in Phase 1–2 BiS sets.

**Still early alpha:** some Forever mechanics are unconfirmed; assumptions are shown in results.
