**Bear threat, corrected item stats and beta build 70450 — v0.37.0 is live**

**Druid (Bear)**
• Bear Form threat bonus is now 50% (was 45%), matching the client.
• Rage from damage taken now ignores armor for Druids too, as it does for Warriors, on Blizzard's rebalanced basis. Feral Tank TPS is up about 17%.

**Items**
• ZG reputation rewards and Arathi Basin vendor weapons now have their item level 60 stats.
• Forever stat ratings now convert correctly: hit was overstated by 25%, and defense was under-counted by more than half. Dodge, parry and block ratings are fixed too.
• Weapons and trinkets that lost their "Chance on hit" and "Use" effects have them back (Sulfuras, Annihilator and others). The item catalog is refreshed to beta build 70450, with 111 new Forever items.
• BiS sets and benchmarks are rebuilt for every spec.

**Hunter**
• Melee Weave keeps a 0.5 sec Auto Shot cost per melee special. If Forever keeps resetting Auto Shot on melee swings, weaving would come out behind plain Survival.
