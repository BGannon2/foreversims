**Paladin BiS, smarter gear sets, faction-correct gear — v0.27.0 is live**

**Paladin**
• Protection and Retribution now have an "⚡ Assumed Forever BiS" button. Their gear picks are weighted by what each stat is actually worth in the Paladin sim; for Protection threat, spell power is the best stat.
• Forever-only gear (like Stalwart Helm) now counts for Paladins. Before, equipping it added nothing.
• Heads up: many pieces in the old Paladin presets (Deathbone, Truestrike and others) have been removed from Forever.

**Better BiS sets for every spec**
• The sim now picks weapons, trinkets and other on-use or proc items, so weapon speed, procs and on-use effects count (Hand of Justice, Earthstrike, Talisman of Ephemeral Power and more).
• Sets gained 2–15% across the board, e.g. Fury +13%, Warrior Protection +15% TPS, Assassination +12%, Protection Paladin +10% TPS.
• Sets no longer include other classes' gear, two items you can only equip one of, dual-wielding on classes that can't, or unreleased raid quest rewards.
• Items requiring level 50+ are now included.

**Alliance / Horde gear**
• Faction-only PvP and battleground gear is restricted to that faction's races.
• Changing race swaps faction gear to the other side's equivalent where one exists (648 of 797 items). Anything without an equivalent is removed and listed.

**Fixes**
• The All Specs BiS button failed to simulate any set that included rings or trinkets.

**Still early alpha:** some Forever mechanics, proc rates and faction gear pairings are unconfirmed. Assumptions are shown in results.
