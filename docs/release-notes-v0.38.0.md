**Healer sims — v0.38.0 is live**

**New: four healer simulators**
• Holy Priest, Restoration Druid, Restoration Shaman and Holy Paladin, each with Forever spell ranks, healing talents, a default build and its own BiS set.
• Healing is measured as HPS until the fight ends: every heal lands on missing health, so your mana over the whole fight is the limit. Rotations pace mana to the fight length.
• The target count is the number of injured allies. Prayer of Healing, Prayer of Mending, Wild Growth, Tranquility, Chain Heal and spread HoTs scale with it.
• New **Healer Comparison** page, alongside DPS and Tank.

**Mana**
• Healers default to Flask of Distilled Wisdom, Brilliant Mana Oil, Mageblood, Nightfin Soup, Major Mana Potions and Demonic Runes.
• Flask of Supreme Power, Greater Arcane Elixir and Brilliant Wizard Oil now add spell damage only, not healing.
• New raid buff option: Innervate from a raid Druid (off by default).
• Mana Spring Totem is now on by default for healers and off for other specs, so caster DPS dips slightly (Balance about −5%).

**Not modelled yet:** downranking, overhealing, Earth Shield, Power Word: Shield and Light's Vigil.
