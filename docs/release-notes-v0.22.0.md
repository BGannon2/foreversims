**Accuracy update — v0.22.0 is live**

This release fixes several issues that were affecting normal runs:
• Warrior talent scaling, rage generation and rage-related threat.
• Hunter ranged attack power, base-mana costs, pet damage and Frenzy; Hunters can now choose no pet.
• Spell hit/crit rolls, bleed damage through armor, and tank defense/crushing-blow calculations.
• Paladin Holy Strike and seal scaling, Holy Wrath’s global cooldown, timed item effects and gear enchants.
• Caster enchant defaults, gear-slot selection and missing item stats.

All 740 spec/race/target comparison profiles have been regenerated with consistent default gear and enchants. We also fixed feedback privacy and admin-link handling.

Validation: 113 tests, 69 browser-engine scenarios, and GitHub CI passed. Live Priest and Paladin results matched the updated benchmarks.

**Still early alpha:** some Forever mechanics and proc rates remain unconfirmed. Unsupported effects and assumptions are shown in results. Default builds are baselines, not proven BiS rankings.
