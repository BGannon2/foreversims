**v0.39.0 is live: Nov 3 development notes**

• Every class, racial and talent change from the Nov 3 Forever development notes is in. The sim now reads the live client (1.60.1.70753) for the reworked Paladin, Mage, Druid, Hunter and Priest trees. Numbers the client doesn't carry yet (Shred, Starfire, Shaman spells and others) are applied from the notes and marked provisional.
• Rogue rework (Shank, Poised, Death Mark, poisons scaling with AP), Fury's Reckless Swings and the Shaman talent changes are built from the notes' text. They'll switch to the real client data once it ships.
• Threat: Defensive Stance +80%, Bear Form +115%, Righteous Fury +33% to all threat. Leader of the Pack and Moonkin Aura give 3% crit and don't stack.
• Paladin: Unsealed Wrath replaces seal twisting; Shieldsworn, Conviction, two-hander and Reverence changes. Retribution 656 → 737 DPS, Protection 443 → 713 TPS.
• Every spec's default talent build is now sim-optimized (34 builds), and BiS sets and benchmarks were rebuilt.
