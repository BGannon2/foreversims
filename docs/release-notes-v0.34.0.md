**Warrior update from the latest Forever beta build — v0.34.0 is live**

**Warrior**
• Bloodthirst: 45% of Attack Power (was 35%).
• Whirlwind now always hits with both weapons.
• Critical auto-attacks give 100% more Rage (was 75%). Unbridled Wrath gives 1 Rage for every weapon type.
• Dual Wield Specialization: +5% off-hand damage and +10% off-hand Rage per rank; it no longer adds hit. New talent Furious Precision gives 4/7/10% off-hand hit.
• Talent trees updated: Precision, Improved Cleave and Toughness are gone; Flurry now requires Death Wish; Raging Blows lowers Cleave and Whirlwind Rage costs by 3.
• Default builds: Fury takes Furious Precision; Protection replaces Toughness with Deep Wounds, Impale and Last Stand.
• Default setups: Fury +9.0%, Arms +4.9% DPS, Protection +3.5% threat.

**Note:** these come from the beta client (build 70374), which the sim follows for now.
