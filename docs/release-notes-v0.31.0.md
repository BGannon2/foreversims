**Forever development notes (Sept 24) applied — v0.31.0 is live**

**Paladin**
• Righteous Fury now adds +60% Holy threat (was +90%).
• Holy Strike: 50% weapon damage + 93 Holy, 10 sec cooldown. Sacred Arbiter is now +20%.
• Vengeance stacks up to 3 times and only boosts Physical and Holy damage.
• Twist of Light also cuts Seal mana costs by 20%, so seal twisting is cheaper.
• Protection: −3.5% threat, +4% DPS. Retribution: +2.9% DPS.

**Tanks**
• New raid buff: Thorns. Each boss swing that lands deals 22 Nature damage back. Adds damage and threat for Bear, Protection Warrior and Protection Paladin. Spell power scaling isn't modeled yet (no published value).

**Mage**
• Hot Streak lasts 20 sec, and Ignite no longer stacks damage bonuses twice. Fire: −3.6% DPS.

**Also**
• Gnome Eureka!: 15 sec window, and the boosted abilities also cost 10% less Energy.
• Elemental Fury and Elemental Alacrity are in their correct talent rows.
• BiS sets and benchmarks rebuilt for the affected specs.
