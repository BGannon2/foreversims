**Forever beta build 70425 (Oct 15 notes) — v0.35.0 is live**

**Racials**
• Human Ruthless: +2% crit. Orc Savage: +1% crit; Blood Fury now a 3 min cooldown.
• Dwarf Earth Fury and Tauren Endurance: +2% critical damage. Skyborne haste 3%.
• Gnome Eureka!: 8 sec, no charge limit. Undead Touch of the Grave: 30% (casters) / 20% chance, 25 sec cooldown.

**Mage**
• Arcane Blast redesign: 1.5 sec cast; each stack adds 15% of Intellect as spell damage for 15 sec. Missile Barrage is 2 ranks; new Arcane Wrath. Arcane +16%.
• Fire Power now scales with Fire effects on the target; Critical Mass goes to 5 ranks; Fire Blast always builds Heating Up.
• Ice Lance vs frozen targets +200% (was +300%), and Winter's Chill no longer adds crit. Frost −10%.

**Paladin**
• Righteous Fury back to +90% Holy threat (Protection +12% threat). Champion of the Light 10/20/30% of Intellect.
• Judgement coefficients 0.429, Consecration 0.042 per tick, Shield Specialization 4% mana, Reckoning stores up to 4 charges.

**Shaman**
• Elemental Mastery returns (faster Lightning Bolt/Lava Burst, no Chain Lightning cooldown). Chain Lightning ~35% cheaper. Stormstrike costs 8% of base mana.
• Grace of Air and Windfury Totem now stack: Enhancement +13%.

**Priest / Warlock**
• Devouring Plague 24 sec cooldown, Shadow Word: Death 18 sec, Improved Mind Blast moved; Shadow Mastery 2% per rank.

**Also:** raid buffs are no longer mutually exclusive, Multi-Shot is instant, default talent builds re-spent where trees changed, and BiS sets and benchmarks rebuilt.
