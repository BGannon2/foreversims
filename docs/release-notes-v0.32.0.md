**Feral Druid rework from the Forever class deep dive — v0.32.0 is live**

**Feral (Cat and Bear)**
• Form attacks now use your equipped weapon: its DPS at a fixed 1.0 sec (Cat) or 2.5 sec (Bear) swing speed. Weapon enchants and procs, weightstones and Windfury Totem now work in form.
• Bear: Primal Bite replaces Mangle (20 Rage, 6 sec cooldown, 100% weapon damage + 77).
• Bear: Lacerate joins the rotation. A stacking bleed (up to 5) that also hits harder per existing stack.
• Cat: Mangle is gone; Claw's bonus damage is raised to 115.
• Thick Hide now also adds armor from Defense skill, and form armor multipliers apply to it.
• Default setups: Bear +7.7% DPS, +1.4% threat. Cat +0.7%.

**Not changed yet:** Tiger's Fury stays until the client removes it, Bear rage still uses the Classic formulas, and Primal Bite and Lacerate threat use Maul's multiplier until there are real numbers.

**Also:** the BiS optimizer now weighs one- and two-handed weapons against each other, Protection Warriors and Paladins always get a one-hander and a shield, and a rebuilt BiS set only replaces the old one if it sims better.
