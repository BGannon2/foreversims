**Updated for Forever client build 1.60.1.70009 — v0.29.0 is live**

**Spells**
• Wrath deals about 50% more damage (92–102 at level 60, up from 62–69). Balance DPS is up a little over 1%.
• Rage of the Farseer now only increases attack speed; it no longer speeds up spellcasting.

**Paladin talents**
• Crusade and Improved Holy Strike were removed from the talent tree. The default Retribution build now puts those 4 points into Champion of the Light (3) and Reverence (1), chosen by simulating every legal placement.
• Two-Handed Weapon Specialization is now 2/4/6% (was 3/6/9%).
• Net effect on the default Retribution setup: about +0.5% DPS.

**Items**
• Premier Grand Marshal's Stave and Premier High Warlord's War Staff lost their Spirit (replaced by Spell Penetration, which the sim doesn't model).
• Black Dragonscale Boots no longer count toward the Black Dragon Mail set.
• Tempestria's Frozen Necklace and Frigid Ring were removed from the game.

Auto BiS sets and the comparison pages are regenerated for the new build.

**Still early alpha:** some Forever mechanics are unconfirmed; assumptions are shown in results.
