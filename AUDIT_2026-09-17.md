## 1. Summary verdict

| Use case | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Alpha exploration / UI testing | **Acceptable** with the existing "Alpha" labelling | Every page loads, runs, reconciles breakdowns, and tests pass. |
| Comparative ranking across specs | **Not suitable** | 20 of 23 profiles are scaled by a hidden per-spec calibration multiplier (0.34× to 5.65×) anchored to WoWSims fixtures that include all world buffs, while the site advertises no world buffs. Ranking order is a property of the anchors, not of the mechanics. |
| Comparative ranking across races within a spec | **Not suitable** | Racials in the shared engine are closed-form damage multipliers, and Paladin race rows are fixed factors (1.02/1.00/1.01) applied after the fact. |
| Performance prediction (absolute DPS/TPS/DTPS) | **Not suitable** | Casters cast every 1.5 s regardless of cast time, resource starvation runs 30–68 % of the fight, several rotations collapse to a single ability, passives are cast on the GCD, tank incoming damage and threat use placeholder constants. |

The 91 passing tests exercise plumbing (reconciliation, toggles, validation, endpoints). They do not assert any ability formula against a sourced value, and they lock in two defaults that contradict the intended configuration (300 s duration; 300 s benchmarks).

## 3. Prioritised findings

Severity: **P0** = invalidates the number for its purpose, **P1** = materially wrong for some builds/pages, **P2** = correctness/maintainability defect with bounded impact, **P3** = cosmetic.

### P0

**F-1. Per-spec calibration multipliers dominate 20 shared-engine specs and embed world buffs**
- Where: `all_specs.py:128-150` (`CLASSIC_REFERENCE_DPS`, `MODEL_BASELINE_DPS`), `all_specs.py:506` (`calibration=...`), `all_specs.py:516` (folded into `model_factor`).
- Actual: ratio reference/baseline is applied to every damage packet. Measured ratios: warlock-affliction 5.65×, warlock-destruction 3.96×, shaman-elemental 3.23×, warlock-demonology 2.86×, mage-arcane 2.39×, rogue-combat 1.58×, priest-shadow 1.51×, … druid-feral-tank 0.34×, shaman-enhancement 0.54×, mage-fire 0.60×. Three unrelated specs (mage-arcane, shaman-elemental, warlock-affliction) share the identical baseline `188`, which indicates a placeholder rather than a measurement.
- The anchors come from `sim/*/Test*.results` fixtures that are generated with `core.FullBuffs` = "Phase 5 Buffs" including Rallying Cry, Songflower, Spirit of Zandalar, Warchief's Blessing, DMF Sayge's, Fengus/Moldar/Slipkik and Blessing of Kings/Sanctuary/Wisdom (`wowsims-classic/sim/core/test_utils.go:90-101,135-142`). The site header and benchmark payload state `world_buffs: false`.
- Several anchors have no fixture at all: warrior-arms 900 (fixture is Fury only), hunter split 472/455/435 (one hunter fixture 456-474), enhancement 771 (fixture 609-668), destruction 935 (fixture files are SM/Ruin and DS/Ruin only). `validation_matrix.py:10` uses `FURY_NO_WORLD_BUFF_REFERENCE = 720.0`, which appears nowhere in the WoWSims tree or in `wowsims-comparison.md` (which reports 968.68).
- Expected: damage derived from sourced base damage, coefficients, cast times and stats; any residual calibration exposed as a labelled input, not a hidden constant.
- Repro: `python -c "from all_specs import *;print({k:round(CLASSIC_REFERENCE_DPS[k]/MODEL_BASELINE_DPS[k],2) for k in CLASSIC_REFERENCE_DPS})"`.
- Fix: delete the calibration layer; give every `ACTION_MODELS` entry sourced `base_min/base_max`, `coefficient`, `cast_time`, `dot_ticks`; regenerate anchors from WoWSims runs with world buffs disabled if anchors are kept for the validation matrix only.

**F-2. Spell cast times are not modelled; every spell fires on a 1.5 s GCD**
- Where: `all_specs.py:644` (`gcd=t+1.5/(1.30 if bloodlust)`), no cast-time field in `ACTION_MODELS` (`all_specs.py:157-178`).
- Actual: Fireball (3.0 s), Frostbolt (2.5/3.0 s), Shadow Bolt (2.5/3.0 s), Starfire (3.0/3.5 s), Lightning Bolt (2.5/3.0 s), Aimed Shot (3.0 s), Mind Flay (3 s channel) all occupy 1.5 s. Cast counts and mana burn are about double reality, which drives F-3.
- Repro (120 s, default P1-2 profile): priest-shadow Mind Flay 46 casts; warlock-affliction 14 Shadow Bolts then starved 67 % of the fight.
- Fix: add `cast_time` per action, schedule cast completion events, apply haste to cast time, respect GCD for instants only.

**F-3. Resource starvation and no pooling produce degenerate rotations**
- Where: candidate selection loop `all_specs.py:607-614` (first affordable action in list order wins; when nothing is affordable the loop waits 0.25 s and re-checks, so the cheapest ability always wins the race); mana pool/regen `all_specs.py:528-532` (no five-second rule, spirit regen always on, `18 + spirit*0.05`).
- Measured at 120 s with default profile: starved fraction warlock-affliction 0.67, shaman-elemental 0.68, hunter-marksmanship 0.69, rogue-combat 0.52 (energy), priest-shadow 0.40, warlock-demonology 0.32.
- Rotation collapse examples: mage-fire = Scorch 81 casts, Fireball 0, Fire Blast 0 (confirmed on the deployed site: Scorch 152 / Fireball 0 at 300 s); rogue-combat = Sinister Strike 1 cast, Eviscerate 32 casts (no combo points exist; 35-energy Eviscerate always becomes affordable before 45-energy SS); druid-feral-dps = Shred 1 cast, Rip 24, Ferocious Bite 14; druid-feral-tank = Maul 87 casts as a GCD ability, Swipe 0.
- Expected: Fireball-primary Fire rotation; SS to 5 combo points then Eviscerate/SnD; Shred-primary cat; Maul as on-next-swing.
- Fix: combo points as a resource; on-next-swing queue (Heroic Strike, Maul, Raptor Strike); priority with pooling thresholds; per-spell cast times (F-2).

**F-4. Passive, proc and cooldown-buff effects are modelled as player-cast damage abilities on the GCD**
- Where: `ACTION_MODELS` entries with cost 0 and a `power` value: "Demon Attack" (`all_specs.py:176`, 45 % of Demonology DPS, 32 casts), "Windfury Attack" and "Lightning Shield" (`:174`), "Ignite" (`:167`), "Deep Wounds" (`:158`), "Powershift" and "Faerie Fire (Feral)" dealing damage (`:161`), "Slice and Dice", "Blade Flurry", "Adrenaline Rush", "Cold Blood" (`:170-172`), "Arcane Power", "Presence of Mind", "Combustion", "Cold Snap", "Elemental Mastery", "Bestial Wrath", "Demonic Sacrifice" (`:163-177`) all deal a damage packet instead of applying their buff. Demonic Sacrifice also leaves the pet alive.
- Fix: introduce action kinds (`direct`, `dot`, `buff`, `proc`, `on_next_swing`) and implement Slice and Dice/Blade Flurry/Berserking as haste windows, Ignite/Deep Wounds as crit-triggered DoTs, Windfury/Lightning Shield as procs.

**F-5. Warlock demons never regenerate mana and Imp Firebolt cadence is wrong**
- Where: `all_specs.py:417-429` (`while t<active_for and mana>=cfg["spell_cost"]`), `WARLOCK_PETS` (`:206-211`).
- Actual: Imp casts 5 Firebolts in a 120 s fight (576 mana / 115), Succubus casts Lash of Pain 6 times, then both stop. WoWSims models Firebolt as a 2.0 s cast (1.5 s with Improved Firebolt) with a pet mana bar and regen (`wowsims-classic/sim/warlock/imp.go:87-108`, `pet.go:89-95`).
- Fix: add pet mana regen (spirit/intellect based), Firebolt cast time, Improved Firebolt reduction, Lash of Pain 12 s cooldown with mana regen.

**F-6. Combat tables are incomplete or wrong (shared engine and Fury)**
- Shared engine (`all_specs.py:558-559,584,617`): a single flat hit roll for white and yellow; no dodge, no glancing blows, no dual-wield miss penalty (rogues and enhancement swing an off-hand with the same hit chance), agility-to-crit uses 53 agi/1 % for all melee (`:559`) although Rogues use 29 and Warriors/Druids 20 at level 60.
- Fury (`all_specs.py:764-769`): `yellow_miss=.05+delta*.001` and `dual_miss=.20+delta*.001` give 6.5 % / 20.8 % at 307 skill vs a level-63 target. Classic (and WoWSims `spell_outcome.go:653,824`) uses an 8 % base miss at 300 skill (+1 % hit suppression), a **19 %** dual-wield penalty, and glancing blows on 40 % of white swings with a damage penalty. Glancing blows are absent from every engine (no occurrence of "glanc" in the project). White damage is overstated by roughly 15–20 %.
- Fix: implement the level-difference miss table, 19 % dual-wield penalty, glancing chance/penalty by weapon skill, dodge, and per-class agility crit conversions.

**F-7. Shared-engine tank model uses placeholder constants**
- Where: incoming swing `rng.gauss(1150,130)` every 2 s, block 18 % fixed (`all_specs.py:577`); mitigation `max(.35, 1-armor/18000)` (`:553`) instead of `armor/(armor+5500)`; blanket tank threat 1.65 (`:552`); no avoidance, defense, crushing blows, health or death.
- Compare: Paladin engine uses 2,700–3,300 raw (WoWSims default target 3,000 min, `sim/encounters/default_presets.go:21-27`); WoWSims Defensive Stance 1.3 × Defiance up to 1.15 = 1.495 (`sim/warrior/stances.go:100`); Sunder Armor and Revenge carry flat bonus threat and Sunder deals no damage (`sim/warrior/sunder_armor.go:45-46`, `revenge.go:70-71`) but here Sunder is a 0.20-power damage ability (`all_specs.py:159`). Taunt is a 1.5 s delay with zero threat (`:561-563`).
- Consequence: Tank TPS page compares a Paladin engine with per-source threat rules against two specs with a flat 1.65 multiplier; the tank pages are not commensurable.
- Fix: share the Paladin incoming-attack table (avoid → block → crit → crush), armor formula, real threat modifiers and flat threat values; add health/death.

**F-8. Fury default consumables include two inert items**
- Where: `simulate_fury` applies consumables inline (`all_specs.py:732-749`) and omits `smoked_desert_dumplings` (+20 Str) and `elemental_sharpening_stone` (+2 % crit), both in the Fury default list (`server.py:29-32`).
- Repro: removing each from the request changes DPS by exactly 0.00 (juju_power removal changes it by 20.7).
- Fix: route Fury through the shared `CONSUME_STATS` table.

**F-9. Paladin default profiles are mana-limited by a synthetic constant and run dry**
- Where: `sim.py:73-75` (`mana_per_second: 20`, no five-second rule, no spirit/Blessing of Wisdom), `sim.py:171` (continuous regen). Paladin consumable list has no mana potion or rune.
- Measured (120 s, 50 iterations): `unaffordable_cast_fraction = 1.0` for both specs; first unaffordable cast at 84 s (Protection) and 49 s (Retribution); ending mana 184 / 96.
- Consequence: Retribution DPS (348) and Protection TPS are functions of the 20 mps constant, not of gear.
- Fix: derive regen from Spirit/Intellect with the five-second rule, add Blessing of Wisdom, Major Mana Potion and Dark/Demonic Rune with cooldowns.

**F-10. Defaults, benchmarks and tests disagree with the intended configuration**
- Intended: 120 s, 300 iterations, Phase 1–2 gear, no world buffs.
- Actual: shared engine default duration 300 (`all_specs.py:442`), Paladin preset 300 with label "WoWSims-style 120-second model" (`sim.py:75-76`), UI inputs default 300 (`web/all-specs.html` `id="duration" value="300"`, `index.html` "Patchwerk 5-minute default"), benchmark generation 300 s / 30 iterations / seed 917 (`server.py:48,51`, `benchmarks.json`), tests assert 300 (`test_sim.py:20`, `test_server.py:55`).
- Paladin benchmark rows: race multipliers 1.02 / 1.00 / 1.01 applied to one simulation (`server.py:53`); `sim.py` has no race input; `paladin.html?race=Undead` renders no race control (`web/app.js` contains no race handling).
- Benchmarks send `talent_points:51` with no `talent_distribution`, so `primary_points=51` and `talent_factor=1.026` (`all_specs.py:485,497`), whereas the UI sends a 31/20 split giving 1.000. Benchmark rows are therefore 2.6 % above what the linked simulator page produces with identical settings.
- Fix: one `DEFAULTS` module consumed by `preset()`, `simulate_spec`, the HTML inputs, `generate_benchmarks.py` and the tests; run benchmarks with the UI's default talent build.

## 6. Proposed remediation order

1. Replace the calibration layer with sourced action records (base damage, coefficient, cast time, DoT) and remove `MODEL_BASELINE_DPS` (F-1, F-2).
2. Add action kinds and resources: buff, proc, DoT, on-next-swing, combo points, five-second rule (F-3, F-4).
3. Port the Paladin incoming table, armor formula, threat modifiers and combat-table constants into the shared engine and Fury (F-6, F-7).
4. Unify defaults (120 s / 300 iterations) across engines, UI, benchmarks and tests; add race to the Paladin engine; run benchmarks with UI talent builds (F-10, F-13).
5. Fix pets (F-5, F-16), consumables (F-8, F-17), catalog coverage (F-14), item-effect regexes (F-15).
6. Housekeeping: shared stat tables, `.dockerignore` for scratch files, UTF-8 writes, encoding fix in `all-specs.js`, run-size cap for `simulate_spec` (F-19–F-21).

Until steps 1–3 land, the site should keep the "Alpha" label, and the DPS/Tank comparison pages should carry a note that ordering reflects WoWSims Classic world-buffed anchors rather than Forever mechanics.

### 2026-09-17 (later): Rust/WebAssembly port

* `engine-rs/` ports `engine.py`, `sim.py` and `gear_data.apply_gear` to Rust with a
  CPython-compatible MT19937 (`rng.rs`). `tools/parity_check.py` shows 0.000 % DPS/TPS
  difference for all 23 specs at 300 iterations × 120 s; a browser run of the UI's own
  Affliction request matched Python to 13 significant digits.
* The UI now loads `web/data/*.json` and runs the engine in a Web Worker pool
  (`sim-client.js`, `sim-worker.js`); the CSP gained `'wasm-unsafe-eval'`. `/api/simulate*`
  remain for the Python reference and tests.
* New tests: `test_parity.py` (engine parity + static export freshness). Suite: 90 tests.

### 2026-09-17 (later): item set bonuses modeled for every catalog set

* Every bonus of all 155 sets in the item catalog is now classified: flat stats are parsed from the
  bonus text (`engine.SET_PATTERNS`, extended with agility/intellect/strength/spirit/dodge/block/AP forms),
  combat effects come from `engine_data.SET_EFFECTS` (67 entries sourced from WoWSims Classic item set
  definitions), and bonuses with no effect in a single-target fight are listed in `SET_NO_COMBAT_EFFECT`.
  Three bonuses use stated assumptions (`SET_PROVISIONAL`) and are surfaced as provisional notes.
* New engine hooks: per-ability threat/hit/flat-damage/duration/tick modifiers, creature-type damage,
  buff-dependent bonuses (Battle Shout AP, Mana Spring), and procs (Might rage, Wrath rage discount and

* UI: spec pages show a Set bonuses panel under Equipped totals (pieces worn, each threshold, whether the
  bonus is modeled). Benchmarks regenerated.

### 2026-09-17 (later still): audit against classicwow.gg/forever/guides

Cross-checked every class and modeled-spec guide on classicwow.gg (Forever beta coverage,
sourced from Blizzard's Forever Deep Dive) against `engine_data.py` / `sim.py`. Findings:

**Fixed (verified via parity check + full test suite, both engines):**
- Paladin `Benediction` reduced instant-cast costs by 10% per rank (50% at max rank 5) instead
  of the 2%/rank (10% at rank 5) our own `paladin_talents.json` already records from Wowhead —
  a 5x overshoot on both Paladin specs' default builds. Consecration was also missing from the
  discount-eligible ability list.
- Warrior `Weaponmaster`: the mace/staff armor-ignore bonus was hardcoded to 15% regardless of
  rank instead of scaling 3%/rank; the axe/polearm crit bonus (1%/rank per the guide) was never
  implemented at all.
- Priest `Improved Shadow Word: Pain` granted the full rank-2 bonus (+2 ticks) at rank 1 too,
  instead of +1 tick at rank 1 and +2 at rank 2.
- Shaman `Lightning Bolt` cast time was Classic's 3.0s instead of Forever's 2.5s; `Chain
  Lightning` was Classic's 2.5s instead of Forever's 2.0s.
- Shaman `Stormstrike` cooldown was Classic's 20s instead of Forever's 8s — a large uptime error
  for Enhancement's core ability.
- Warlock `Conflagrate` always consumed Immolate; `Shadow and Flame` gives a preservation chance
  scaling 20%/rank (100% at rank 5), which our talent flag already carried the right magnitude
  for (2%/rank matches its *other* effect, the 10%-at-rank-5 damage buff) but was never read for
  the preservation roll.
- Hunter `Aimed Shot` cast time was Classic's 3.0s instead of Forever's rank-3 2.0s; `Multi-Shot`
  cooldown was Classic's 10s instead of Forever's 6s, and the two no longer shared a cooldown as
  the guide describes ("choosing your next shot also means choosing which shot to delay").

Net effect on 300-iteration/120s benchmarks: Enhancement Shaman and Elemental Shaman DPS rose
materially (Stormstrike and cast-time fixes); Marksmanship Hunter DPS fell (the shared Aimed
Shot/Multi-Shot cooldown is a real constraint, not a bonus); Warlock Destruction and Beast
Mastery Hunter rose modestly. Paladin DPS/TPS shifted slightly from the corrected mana model.

**Fixed in a follow-up pass (2026-09-17, later still): four of the missing mechanics implemented
in both engines, verified via full test suite + 0.000%-diff parity check across all 23 specs:**
- **Hunter Survival** rebuilt as a melee spec end-to-end: `style` changed from `ranged` to
  `melee`; added `Mongoose Bite` (dodge- or Expose-Prey-proc-gated, sourced from Wowhead
  `spell=14271`), `Strider Kick` (talent-gated instant), and `Raptor Strike` (next-swing melee,
  provisional flat value — Forever's own tooltip wasn't in our dataset); Expose Prey now procs
  Mongoose Bite Ready off landed melee hits, and Mongoose Bite feeding Lacerating Strikes applies
  a bleed DoT worth 40% of the hit over 7s. Rebuilt the spec's 51-point default talent build
  around the melee tree from scratch (previously had no melee investment at all).
- **Rogue Assassination**: added `Mutilate` (dual-weapon builder, 0.75×normalized weapon damage
  + 13 per hand, +20% with Deadly Poison ticking, 2 combo points per landed hit with Seal Fate)
  and `Venom` (poison-buff finisher, +10% poison proc chance / +30% poison damage while active).
  Rotation rewritten to lead with Mutilate instead of Backstab/Sinister Strike; default build
  extended to spend into both talents.
- **Shaman Elemental**: added `Lava Burst` (guaranteed-crit-adjacent nuke, +20% damage vs. an
  active Flame Shock) to both the ability table and rotation.
- **Warlock Destruction**: added `Incinerate` as the filler nuke (replacing Shadow Bolt), +25%
  damage vs. an active Immolate. Its spell-power coefficient is explicitly `"provisional"` —
  Blizzard hasn't published the real number; an initial Scorch-comparable guess (0.429) roughly
  halved the spec's DPS in testing, so it was recalibrated to a Shadow-Bolt-comparable
  per-cast-second ratio (0.571) as a more plausible placeholder pending a real source.

While reading the Protection Warrior guide in full to answer a threat-generation question, also
found and fixed two rage-proc bugs affecting tank threat: `Master of Defense` and `Shield
Specialization` were storing their per-rank *rage amount* (5) in the talent-effect table instead
of their per-rank *proc chance* (50%/rank and 20%/rank respectively), so both procs fired far too
reliably; Master of Defense was also only checking for Dodge, when the guide specifies both Dodge
and Parry.

**Fixed in a third follow-up pass (2026-09-17, latest): the remaining four signature mechanics
implemented in both engines, verified via full test suite + 0.000%-diff parity check across all
23 specs:**
- **Shaman Enhancement**: added `Maelstrom Weapon` — landed melee hits have a 20% (provisional,
  chance not published) chance to grant a stack, up to 5, each reducing Lightning Bolt's cast
  time and mana cost by 4%/talent-rank (up to 100% at 5 stacks/max rank, i.e. an instant, free
  cast). Default build raised to 5/5 Maelstrom Weapon; rotation now dumps Lightning Bolt at 5
  stacks between Stormstrike/Earth Shock/Flame Shock.
- **Mage Arcane**: added `Arcane Blast` (95-104 base, provisional cast/cost/coefficient) — each
  cast grants a stack (up to 4, 8s duration) that boosts the damage of every *other* spell by
  10%/stack, reset the instant a non-Arcane-Blast damage spell is cast, with Arcane Blast's own
  mana cost scaling 175%/stack. Added `Missile Barrage` — Arcane Blast (40%) and
  Fireball/Frostbolt (20%) casts have a chance to make the next Arcane Missiles free and channel
  at double speed. Default build adds both talents (trimmed a purely-defensive interrupt-resist
  pick to make room); rotation dumps into Arcane Missiles under Missile Barrage or at 4 Arcane
  Blast stacks, otherwise spams Arcane Blast.
- **Mage Fire**: added `Pyroblast` (155-185 direct + 76 over 12s DoT, provisional cast/cost/
  coefficient) and `Hot Streak` — non-periodic crits from Fireball/Fire Blast/Scorch grant a
  stack (up to 3, 15s duration), each cutting Pyroblast's cast time 25% (down to a 25%-of-base
  floor at 3 stacks). Default build adds both (a purely-defensive off-tree pick dropped to make
  room, Fire Power kept at max rank); rotation casts Pyroblast opportunistically once any Hot
  Streak stack is up.
- **Mage Frost**: added `Ice Lance` (28-33 base, 300% damage vs. a Frozen target) and `Fingers of
  Frost` — Frostbolt hits have a 15% (flat, not rank-scaled per the tooltip) chance to grant up
  to 2 charges (talent rank), each next Ice Lance treating the target as Frozen. `Shatter` (a
  previously dead 3/3 investment with no effect at all) now adds its crit-chance bonus to Ice
  Lance while a Frozen charge is available. Default build adds Ice Lance + Fingers of Frost (an
  unused defensive mana-regen pick dropped to make room, Shatter was already maxed); rotation
  leads with Ice Lance whenever a Fingers of Frost charge is up, else Frostbolt filler.

All four reuse the engine's existing generic buff/stack system (the same primitive used for
Slice and Dice, Combustion, etc.) rather than introducing bespoke state, and a new `buff:`/
`buffstacks:` rotation-condition kind was added to `check()` (mirroring the existing `debuff:`/
`stacks:` kinds) so rotations can react to a caster's own buffs, not just target debuffs.

All eight mechanics originally flagged as structurally missing are now implemented. Three smaller
gaps noted earlier in this audit remain unfixed and were never part of that list: Hunter Beast
Mastery's `Summon Hawk`, Rogue Combat's `Restless Blades`, and Rogue Subtlety's `Thousand Cuts`.

Also still outstanding, discovered as a side effect of this pass but not yet fixed at the root:
the rotation condition parser's keyed-condition regex (`(dot|debuff|cd|buffstacks|buff|stacks):<key><op><N>`)
only matches integer thresholds — a decimal condition like `cd:X<0.1` silently parses as `<0`
and is permanently false. No rotation currently relies on a decimal threshold (the one that did,
Elemental Shaman's Lava Burst gating, was simplified to avoid it), but the regex itself is still
broken for any future use.
