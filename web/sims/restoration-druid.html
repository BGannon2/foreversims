Restoration Druid Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Restoration Druids in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.
