Protection Warrior Simulator

A free tank simulator for Protection Warriors in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Rotation

A threat rotation first: Shield Slam, Revenge, Sunder Armor upkeep, Execute, Cleave once 2+ targets are up, Heroic Strike as a rage dump. Damage output is a side effect of holding aggro, not the priority.

Threat

Defensive Stance grants +30% threat (×1.3), and Shield Slam/Revenge/Sunder Armor/Cleave/Heroic Strike all add large flat threat bonuses on top of their damage-based threat - the most complete tank threat kit of the two shared-engine tanks.
