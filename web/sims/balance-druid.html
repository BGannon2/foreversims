Balance Druid Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Balance Druids in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Current Balance Druid DPS by race

Default setup: level 60, Classic Anniversary Phase 1–2 gear, default talents, full raid buffs and consumables, one level-63 target (3,731 armor), 120 seconds, 300 iterations. Open the sim to change gear, talents, race or encounter.

Rotation

Priority: keep Moonfire and Insect Swarm up (spread across every available target once one is already active, rather than refreshing on the same target), Starfire as the main nuke, Wrath as filler. Eclipse lets Wrath charges shorten Starfire's cast time.

Threat

Moonkin Form carries no threat modifier (×1.0) and Balance has no dedicated threat tool, so its TPS tracks its DPS almost 1:1.
