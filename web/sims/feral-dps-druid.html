Feral DPS Druid Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Feral DPS Druids in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Rotation

Priority: Berserk on cooldown, Tiger's Fury for its damage bonus, Shifting Power to turn Mana into Energy at 50 Energy or less, Ferocious Bite at 5 combo points with a healthy Rip up, Rip to apply the bleed, Shred as the builder, Claw as a fallback when Shred is unusable.

Threat

Cat Form carries the largest threat penalty modeled (×0.71), so Feral DPS's TPS trails its DPS more than any other melee spec.
