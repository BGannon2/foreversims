Arms Warrior Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Arms Warriors in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Rotation

Priority: Death Wish on cooldown, Execute below 20% health, Overpower after a dodge, Mortal Strike, Whirlwind, Spearing Strike against Giants/Dragonkin at high rage, Heroic Strike queued whenever rage allows.

Threat

Battle Stance carries a flat -20% threat penalty (×0.8) on every hit, so Arms' TPS trails its DPS more than a threat-neutral spec's would.
