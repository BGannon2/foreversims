Holy Paladin Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Holy Paladins in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Open the Holy Paladin sim

Rotation

Healing: Divine Favor and Holy Shock on cooldown, Holy Light while mana is ahead of the fight's pace, Flash of Light otherwise. Illumination refunds mana on crits.

Threat

No threat modelled for healing.
