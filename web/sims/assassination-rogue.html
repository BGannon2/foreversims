Assassination Rogue Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Assassination Rogues in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Rotation

Priority: Thistle Tea when low on Energy, Cold Blood at 5 combo points, Venom to buff poison effects, Slice and Dice upkeep, Eviscerate at 5 combo points, Mutilate as the main combo-point builder.

Threat

Rogues carry a class-wide -29% threat modifier (×0.71) regardless of stance or talents - the only class-level (not stance-level) threat penalty modeled - so all three Rogue specs' TPS trails their DPS by roughly the same margin.
