Restoration Shaman Simulator

A free DPS simulator for Restoration Shamans in World of Warcraft: Forever. It models Forever's talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear, and runs thousands of fights in your browser. Nothing to install and no sign-up.

Open the Restoration Shaman sim

Rotation

Healing: Nature's Swiftness on cooldown, Mana Tide Totem when mana falls behind, Riptide on cooldown, Healing Wave otherwise. Chain Heal is off by default: with Forever values (490 base, 50% per jump) it heals less per second and per mana than Healing Wave even with 3 injured allies. No downranking yet, so Shaman can run dry before the fight ends.

Threat

No threat modelled for healing.
