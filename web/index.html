What is Forever Sims?

Forever Sims is a free simulator for World of Warcraft: Forever. Pick a class and spec to sim your DPS, or your threat as a tank, with Forever's new talent trees, reworked abilities and racials on top of Classic Anniversary gear. Change gear, enchants, talents, race, raid buffs and consumables, and run thousands of fights right in your browser. Nothing to install, no sign-up.

The simulators are updated as each Forever build changes class and talent data, and the DPS and tank comparisons rank every spec and race on the same default setup.

Model status

Every simulator is labeled Alpha. All specs run on event-driven models with sourced Classic Anniversary ability data, Forever talents and racials, and no calibration multipliers. Values remain Alpha until Forever combat logs confirm the Forever-specific changes.
